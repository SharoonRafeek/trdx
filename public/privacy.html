Privacy notice

What this website collects, why, and what happens to it. Last updated October 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Traversion Dynamics. If you have a question about this notice, get in touch through the contact page.

What we collect

The only way this site collects personal information is the contact form. If you use it, we receive:

  • your name and email address;
  • your organization and role, if you choose to give them;
  • the topic you selected and the message you write.

Please do not include classified or sensitive operational details in the form.

How we use it

We use these details only to read and reply to your request. We do not use them for advertising and we do not sell them.

Who receives it

Your message is passed to our team by email through a mail service we operate or contract. The website does not keep a copy in a database.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and images are served from our own domain, so your browser does not contact third-party servers to display the pages.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as it is needed to respond to you and to keep a reasonable record of the conversation.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details you sent us by getting in touch through the contact page.

Changes

If we change how this site handles personal information, we will update this page and its date.
